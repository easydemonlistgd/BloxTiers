Rules

Please read the rules before participating in rankings.

01

Be Respectful

Treat players, testers, staff, and other members respectfully. Harassment and unnecessary toxicity are not allowed.

02

No Cheating

Cheats, unfair modifications, macros, or other tools that provide an unfair advantage are not allowed.

03

Valid Results Only

Submitted results must be legitimate and must follow the requirements for the relevant kit.

04

No Fake Results

Do not submit fake, edited, fabricated, or otherwise misleading results.

05

Evidence

Staff may request proof of a result. Players should provide the requested evidence when necessary.

06

Staff Decisions

Staff may review, remove, or correct rankings when a result does not meet the requirements.

07

Use Common Sense

Not every situation can be covered by a written rule. Use common sense and follow staff instructions when necessary.
